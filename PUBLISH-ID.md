# Publish Brainrot Bazaar ke Roblox — panduan buat user

Bounty: make.fun `roblox-brainrot-01` — "Make the best AI Roblox brainrot game", pot $1.000.
Deadline: **Jumat 20 Nov 2026, 21:30 WIB** (2026-11-20T14:30:00Z).
Status game: sudah jadi — `game.rbxl` (28.666 byte, rebuilt 19 Nov 01:24 WIB dari
`src/` lengkap: 21 brainrot, 7 rarity, conveyor, base, steal, rebirth, tsunami 90 detik).

## Yang kamu butuhkan

- File `game.rbxl` dari repo ini (Muse yang kirim ke kamu).
- Roblox Studio di Windows/Mac + akun Roblox kamu (sudah dikonfirmasi: akun ada,
  umur > 2 hari, lolos age check — jalur publish aman).

## Langkah publish (di komputermu)

1. Buka Roblox Studio, **File > Open from File**, pilih `game.rbxl`.
2. **File > Publish to Roblox As... > Create New Experience**.
   - Nama: `Brainrot Bazaar`
   - Deskripsi (boleh copy-paste):
     > Brainrot Bazaar — a Steal a Brainrot style game. 21 original brainrots walk the
     > conveyor past 8 bases. Buy with E, slap thieves with F, lock your base with L,
     > rebirth with R for x2 income. Chase Golden / Diamond / Rainbow mutations and
     > survive the tsunami every 90 seconds.
   - Visibility: **Public**. Publish.
3. Di Creator Dashboard (create.roblox.com) > Creations > experience kamu:
   - Upload **icon** dan **thumbnail** (pakai satu screenshot gameplay asli sebagai thumbnail).
4. Mainkan gamenya dari Roblox (bukan cuma di Studio), lalu ambil **3 screenshot gameplay asli**:
   - 1) awal game / conveyor berjalan, 2) lagi beli / main, 3) aksi (curi / tsunami).
   - Salah satu screenshot harus sama dengan thumbnail yang di-upload di atas.
5. Kirim balik ke Muse: **link place** (contoh: `https://www.roblox.com/games/<place_id>/...`),
   **3 file screenshot PNG**, dan (kalau sempat) **place_id + universe_id**.
